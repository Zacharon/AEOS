# Knowledge, context and authority

Knowledge acquisition and project writeback are separate operations. A returned artifact does not automatically become trusted knowledge.

| Stage | Meaning | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | Preserved source evidence with provenance | Collection does not make source text an instruction |
| Silver | Proposed synthesis with target, diff, uncertainty and checks | Agents may draft and verify; proposal remains pending |
| Owner decision | Accept, revise, reject or defer the exact proposal | Human-controlled record; agents cannot fill it |
| Gold | Approved knowledge applied after freshness and checkpoint checks | Separate authorized application; no autonomous agent promotion |

An authorized controller may execute a deterministic apply after the owner's exact decision and separate instruction. Agents cannot choose the decision or promote pending material. If source, target or payload changes, inspect and prepare a fresh proposal rather than editing evidence to fit.

## Select context in layers

ICM, Interpretable Context Methodology, groups context by purpose: entry and routing; operating rules; project or stage facts; stable references; working artifacts. Read across those layers only as the task requires. A stage contract names one transformation, its inputs, outputs, allowed actions, verification and writeback targets.

A context manifest records the chosen sources, why each matters, exclusions and freshness observations. It is a derived selection, never a second source of project truth. Source, concept and synthesis notes retain provenance and uncertainty; project records hold current facts. Stable topics route to the owning record rather than a copied global summary.

When answering, separate facts from inference and conflicts. Cite the evidence actually inspected. Missing or stale material remains unknown until reconciled.
